# Status

[Version 1.0.0](https://github.com/DannyExperiments/recognizing-m-identifiability/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · 27 November 2026 · [Version DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24570842)

Recognizing ordinary M-identifiability of a factor analysis graph is NP-complete when matching witnesses have unrestricted size. This answers the ordinary half of Conjecture 5.3 of Sturma, Kranzlmueller, Portakal and Drton.

The internal mathematical review covered the exact characterization, the reduction, and the factor-parameter algorithm. External peer review and formal verification have not been conducted. See the [mathematical review](audits/public_safe_reports/MATHEMATICS.md) and [source comparison](audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE.md).
