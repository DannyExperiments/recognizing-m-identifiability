# Q403: Unrestricted M-identifiability is NP-complete

We prove the ordinary M-identifiability part of [Sturma–Kranzlmueller–Portakal–Drton Conjecture 5.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.02986v2#S5). We do not claim the extended M-identifiability part, which allows an additional criterion.

## A local exact-matching lemma

In the current graph after deleting previously certified latents, assume the proposed observed pivot v has the single parent h. Write N(U) for the latent parents incident to at least one vertex in U. A matching consists of disjoint triples (u,g,w), with u in U, w in W, both children of the distinct latent g. Observed endpoints are disjoint because U and W are disjoint. With v added to both sides, any complete matching must use the path (v,h,v): both copies can only use h, and two distinct paths cannot both use h. Thus v certifies h exactly when some base U,W have a perfect matching but no perfect matching after deletion of h.

An equivalent witness is a matching using h, with disjoint endpoint sets U,W avoiding v, whose set of centers is *exactly* N(U)∩N(W). Such a witness plainly blocks any matching after removal of h. Conversely, take any base witness of size k, and form a unit-capacity matching network with separate layers U, split latents other than h, and W. Only source arcs to U, latent split arcs, and W arcs to the sink have unit capacity; other arcs have capacity >k. By the integral flow and min-cut theorem, a cut of capacity <k exists. Let U' be the left vertices on the source side and W' the right vertices on the sink side. The cut counts at least k−|U'| source arcs, k−|W'| sink arcs, and every common latent of U',W' other than h; each such latent has its in-copy on the source side and out-copy on the sink side. Consequently |N(U')∩N(W') except h| < |U'|+|W'|−k. At least |U'|+|W'|−k paths of a fixed perfect base matching have both endpoints in U',W'. Their distinct centers are all common latent parents. Since adding back h raises the count of possible centers by at most one, both counts must equal |U'|+|W'|−k, and h must be used. Keep just these paths. Their centers remain common, and no new common parent can arise by restriction. This gives the desired equivalent witness. Below call a matching with exactly all its common parents an exact matching.

## Construction from 3-CNF satisfiability

Use a nonempty formula with clauses of at most three literals, and with both polarities of each variable occurring. This does not affect hardness: add the tautological two-literal clause x∨¬x for each variable; trivial empty inputs can be handled separately. Names of all introduced vertices are distinct unless explicitly the same below. We describe a factor graph by the child set of each latent node.

First describe formula latents g_1,…,g_m, where m≥1, and their original children. They use two observed anchors a,b.
- For each variable create observed literal vertices X_i and N_i. A variable latent has children a,b,X_i,N_i.
- For every clause j, create an observed reference spare c_j and a separate observed spare s_{j,l} for each literal occurrence l. A clause latent has children a,b,c_j and all its occurrence spares.
- Each reference spare has its own equality latent with children a,c_j. Each occurrence spare has its own equality latent with children s_{j,l} and the literal vertex for that occurrence (X_i or N_i). Equalities are separate latent nodes.

Now set K=3m. Create observed pairs a_i,b_i for 0≤i≤K and identify the formula anchors a=a_K and b=b_K. Add an observed v and a latent h with children {v,a_0,b_0}. For stages 1≤i≤K, add:
- relay latent r_i with children {a_(i−1),b_(i−1),a_i,b_i};
- lock latent t_i with children {a_(i−1),a_i,b_i,z_i,y_i}, where z_i,y_i are new private observed vertices.

For formula latent g_j and its three corresponding stages i=3j−2,3j−1,3j, add z_i as a child of g_j. Add one filler latent F_j with exactly those three y_i as children. In particular each z_i has exactly parents t_i and g_j; y_i has exactly parents t_i and F_j. This completes the graph. All observed vertices other than v have at least two parents: this is immediate for the anchors, relay vertices and private pairs, and follows from equality latents for spares and the both-polarities condition for literals. Initially v is the sole eligible pivot and h the sole latent that could be certified first. The construction has linear size in the formula and its occurrences.

## If h can be certified, the formula is satisfiable

Use an exact matching containing h, with endpoints colored left and right by U,W. The h-path occupies a_0,b_0 in opposite colors. Inductively if a_(i−1),b_(i−1) are occupied in opposite colors then r_i is a common parent and must be a center. The previous pair is occupied, leaving only a_i,b_i for it, again in opposite colors. This forces every relay pair, including a,b. Now each t_i is common because a_i,b_i have opposite colors. All relay pair observations are already occupied, so its two endpoints must be its own private pair z_i,y_i. Every added port of every formula latent is occupied by these locks.

Each variable latent is common from a,b, so it must use its only two still available children X_i,N_i, making them opposite. Declare the color of b true. If a selected spare has color opposite its reference (a for a reference spare, the literal vertex for an occurrence spare), its equality latent is common and must be used. But its literal or anchor reference and all its added ports are occupied, leaving only one vertex; this is impossible. Thus selected spares inherit their reference colors. A clause latent is common from a,b and must use two oppositely colored spares of its own. Its reference spare, if selected, has the color of a. At least one of the two endpoints must then be an occurrence spare of color b. Its literal is true. Every clause is satisfied.

## A satisfying assignment certifies all latents

Color each a_i left and b_i right, match a_0,b_0 through h, and a_i,b_i through r_i. Use each variable latent to match its literal vertices, true on the right. For each clause choose one true occurrence, and match that occurrence spare (right) with its reference spare (left) through the clause latent. No other spares are selected. For the original formula graph the variable and clause latents are common and used; equality latents are not common because their selected original children are monochromatic. For each group of three stages attached to g_j, put all z_i in the color of its selected original children if g_j is not common (choose either if none); if g_j is common, choose either color. Put all corresponding y_i in the opposite color, so the filler F_j is not common. Match each private pair through its lock t_i. The new children did not make any dormant formula center common. All and only common centers are used, so this exact matching certifies h at v.

Proceed i=1,…,K. After h and the preceding relays and locks have been certified, b_(i−1) has only parent r_i. Restrict the original exact matching to centers still unresolved, deleting the paths with certified centers along with their endpoints. It is still exact: every retained center has its retained endpoints; no new common parent appears by restriction. The pivot b_(i−1) is not retained, since its path was through h or r_(i−1). The matching still uses r_i, so certifies it. Now a_(i−1) has only parent t_i, and the same argument with the further restricted matching certifies t_i. This verifies both local checks at each stage without any other certifications.

Finally every g_j has three z children with sole parent g_j after the locks have been removed; choose one as pivot and the other two as singleton matching sides. Each F_j has the same property with its three y children. Thus all formula and filler latents can be certified. Unsatisfiable formulas admit no initial certification by the previous direction, proving hardness.

## Membership in NP and scope

A certificate lists an order of all latent nodes and a pivot and two matching sets for each. Each check uses a split-vertex integral max-flow computation for the base sets, then for the augmented sets (or equivalently the base sets with the single parent removed); hence it is polynomial-time verifiable. The reduction addresses the exact unbounded-set criterion of Definitions 4.1 and 4.7 and Conjecture 5.3 (ordinary M-identifiability only). The source's fixed-bound algorithm does not cover this reduction, whose witnesses can grow. No claim about generic identifiability decision complexity or extended M-identifiability follows here. No priority claim.
